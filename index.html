II.  Selected Projects

Eight system-identification, control and modeling projects, 2023–2026.

Actuator System Identification & Standing-Balance RL for a 30-DoF Humanoid

CompletedResearch internship · Apr–Aug 2026 · MOGA Humanoid Robotics, Wuhu

  • Identified actuator dynamics (armature, direction/velocity-dependent friction & damping, communication delay) for 7 custom motor types on a 30-DoF humanoid, using the PACE framework with a CMA-ES optimization loop in Isaac Lab against chirp-sweep excitation data.
  • Built a multi-joint chirp-sweep excitation pipeline in ROS 2 Control, driving independent frequency, amplitude and PD gains per joint set while locking non-excited joints at stand pose with high-stiffness PD control.
  • Showed that combined multi-joint identification outperforms isolated single-joint identification on load-bearing joints, reducing average on-robot tracking RMSE relative to non-identified defaults.
  • Trained a PPO standing-balance policy (Isaac Lab, 4096 parallel environments) that rejects upper-body disturbances, with domain-randomization ranges centered on the identified actuator parameters, narrowing the sim-to-real gap for deployment.
  • ROS 2
  • Isaac Lab
  • MuJoCo
  • PyTorch

  1. Physics-Informed Identification of Nonlinear Dynamic Systems. Research project, Paris-Saclay, Dec 2025–Mar 2026. Modeled bioprocess dynamics as black-box and gray-box systems, comparing a pure-ODE baseline against Physics-Informed Neural Network formulations; iterated the PINN loss and architecture across several versions, adding a weighted mean-square-error loss and a Bayesian search over the physics-loss weight λ. Keywords: MATLAB, PINN, Bayesian optimization.
  2. Force-Feedback Steering Wheel: Dynamics Modeling & Embedded Control. Academic project, LISV, Paris-Saclay, Apr–Jun 2025. Derived the handle's rotational dynamics and built the Simulink model linking commanded torque to angular response; designed a transparent-mode force-feedback loop and an active force-tracking mode; instrumented the handle with bonded strain gauges and an HX711 load-cell front end read from STM32F10x firmware. Keywords: Simulink, STM32/C, HX711 load cell, strain gauges.
  3. Mobile Robot Control via Discrete-Event & Linear Systems. Academic project, Toulouse III, Feb–Jun 2024. Combined a discrete-event state machine (AED) for mode and sequencing logic with PID controllers (ASL) tuned from the identified system dynamics; modeled and validated the combined architecture in Simulink. Keywords: Simulink, MATLAB, state machines, PID.
  4. Underwater Image Semantic Segmentation (SUIM). Coursework, machine learning. Benchmarked DeepLab v3 and U-Net on the SUIM dataset (1,525 train/validation and 110 held-out test images across 8 classes spanning divers, reefs, robots, wrecks and sea-floor), training and evaluating both models in PyTorch. Keywords: Python, PyTorch, DeepLab v3, U-Net.
  5. Statistical Analysis of Meteorological Time Series. Coursework, applied statistics. Ran a preliminary exploratory study of a weather time-series dataset, applied Principal Component Analysis (ACP) to reduce and interpret the variable space, and compared hierarchical clustering (CAH) and k-means to segment the series into regimes. Keywords: Python, scikit-learn, Jupyter.
  6. Trapped Electric Charges in PEN Polymer Films. Experimental study, Toulouse III, Oct–Dec 2023. Studied the energetic distribution of trapped electrical charges in PEN thin-film polymers using the Laser-Induced Modulation Method (LIMM) and Pressure/Surface-potential Decay (PSD) techniques. Keywords: LIMM, PSD, experimental electrostatics.
  7. Point-Discharge Effect: Electric Field Study. Coursework, Toulouse III. Measured and compared point-to-plane versus plane-to-plane electrode configurations for corona (point-discharge) effects, processing the acquired field data with custom C programs. Keywords: C, experimental electrostatics.
